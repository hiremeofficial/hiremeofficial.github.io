Questions

Good to know

Who is hire.me for?

Any college student or recent grad who has more going on than their resume shows. Business, engineering, law, media, science, design: if you've built, led or made anything outside class, there's a story worth telling.

What exactly do I get?

A portfolio designed around you, live as one link you can share anywhere, plus a matching resume in the same style. With the DIY workshop, you leave knowing how to build your own.

How is this different from a template or an AI tool?

Templates and AI make everyone look the same. We start with who you are, choose what's worth showing and art-direct the whole thing, so nobody else's portfolio looks like yours.

Will it still feel like me?

Yes. We do the writing and the design, but it all starts from our chat with you, and you approve every draft before it's final.

Where can I use it?

Job and internship applications, LinkedIn, your Instagram bio, your email signature, grad school applications. Anywhere you want someone to remember you.

What do I need to have ready?

Nothing polished. Links, photos, screenshots, old files, even rough notes. Sorting through it is our job.

Who owns the portfolio?

You do. You can edit it whenever you like, and nothing goes public until you say so.
